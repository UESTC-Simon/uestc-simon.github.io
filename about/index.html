Shiteng Lu / 路仕腾

Following questions across disciplines.

I am an undergraduate studying mathematics and physics at the University of Electronic Science and Technology of China (UESTC), and a research intern in Ziyin Liu’s group at Tsinghua University. My research experience spans language model safety, spiking neural networks, and multimodal evaluation. My recent work also explores CAD geometry evaluation and neuron segmentation.

I like research that turns an interesting question into a careful experiment—and leaves room to change our minds when the evidence asks us to.

Questions I’m following

  • Language model safety
  • Efficient neural networks
  • Learning & memory

I’m also increasingly curious about neuroscience, and hope to explore how ideas about learning and memory can inform our understanding of intelligent systems.
